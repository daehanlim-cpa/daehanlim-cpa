## Daehan Lim, CPA

Senior Forward Deployed Engineer at Deloitte. I build production data and AI systems inside regulated financial environments.

I started as a CPA doing AML, BSA, and OFAC controls assessments — sitting across from Chief Compliance Officers. Then I moved to the other side of the table and started building the systems instead. That combination is most of what I do now: I own enterprise engagements from executive discovery through production deployment, which means I'm in the stakeholder conversation shaping an ambiguous problem *and* in the repository building the thing that solves it.

Seven years in, across Deloitte and EY, working with government pension funds, regional banks, and digital asset platforms.

### What I work on

- **GenAI and agentic systems in production** — RAG pipelines and LLM workflows that carry real operational weight, not demos. Continuous control monitoring, automated quality assessment of analyst dispositions against scattered policy.
- **Enterprise data modernization** — Snowflake and Databricks platforms, ingestion through governance to analytics. Replacing manual Excel reporting with orchestrated pipelines that hold up to audit.
- **Governance that survives scrutiny** — metadata management, lineage, data quality testing in CI/CD, DEV/STG/PROD separation with approval gates. The unglamorous work that decides whether a platform is trustworthy.
- **Regulatory and audit execution** — AML framework and model validation, sanctions screening assessment, liquidity reporting controls. Audit-ready findings presented to CCOs and Chief Audit Executives.
